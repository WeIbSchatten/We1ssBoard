**Overview** is the first page after login. It answers three questions at a
glance: is everything running, who is using the server, and where the traffic
goes. Every card links to the page where you fix or change what it shows.

## Key figures

The row of tiles at the top shows:

- **Online now** — clients with traffic in the last poll, out of all clients;
- **Traffic today** — upload plus download across all inbounds since midnight;
- **Inbounds** — how many inbounds exist and how many carry clients right now;
- **Nodes** — nodes online out of the enabled ones;
- **Needs attention** — open problems; the subtitle counts the ones that need
  fixing now.

## Traffic

The chart stacks upload and download for **24 hours**, **7 days** or **30
days**. The panel stores hourly totals for 32 days and monthly totals forever
(see [Database](/docs/reference/database)), so the history survives restarts and
traffic resets.

## Needs attention

A list of what is wrong, each with a **Fix** button that opens the right page:

- Xray is not running or failed to start;
- CPU, memory or disk is running high;
- a node is offline, or Xray is not running on it;
- an outbound is down (see [Outbound health](#outbound-health));
- clients ran out of traffic or time, or expire soon.

**All good** means the list is empty.

## Inbounds and top clients

**Inbounds** lists each inbound with its protocol, how many clients are online
on it and its traffic. **Top clients** ranks clients by usage. Both cards switch
between:

- **This period** — usage since the last reset, the same counters the traffic
  limits use;
- **All time** — usage including traffic before resets.

All-time totals start from the counters found when you update to 1.10.1;
traffic before that is not known to the panel.

## Connected now

The clients connected right now, with the inbound each one uses. The inbound is
exact when the access log is on; see
[Clients → Active connection](/docs/config/clients#active-connection).

## Nodes

Every node with its state: online, online but Xray is not running, offline or
disabled. A node [hidden from the dashboard](/docs/operations/multi-node#hide-a-node-from-the-dashboard)
stays in this card, but its clients and inbounds leave the other cards.

## Outbound health

The panel checks its proxy outbounds itself, so the card works without an Xray
observatory in the config:

- every proxy outbound is probed every **10 minutes**;
- a failing one is rechecked every minute until it answers again;
- it is shown as **Down** only after the number of failures in a row set in
  **Notifications** (3 by default), and the panel sends an outbound-down
  notification at that moment.

Outbounds an observatory already watches are probed too, but do not send a
second notification.

## Events

The latest events since the panel started: outbounds going down and back up, Xray
crashes, nodes going offline and back online, high CPU or memory, logins. They
are kept in memory, so a panel restart clears the list.
